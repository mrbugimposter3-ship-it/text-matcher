How it works: the app picks your text's most distinctive words, asks Gutendex (the Project Gutenberg catalog) for related books, downloads their full text, and ranks them by shared word sequences. Checking all ~75,000 books live isn't possible from a phone, so it checks the most relevant ones. Gutenberg doesn't allow direct downloads from web pages, so texts go through a public proxy.
